# COM7022 Marking Rubric (supplied by student, 2026-10-24)

Single holistic scale applied to the portfolio.

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 90-100 | Exemplary technical understanding and judgement. Exceptional application of working process and techniques. |
| 80-89 | An outstanding understanding of the processes involved when conducting an ML investigation. Data importing, pre-processing and descriptive statistics have all been completed correctly. Correct ML methodology has been applied. All processes and solutions are correct. |
| 70-79 | An excellent understanding of the processes involved when conducting a ML investigation. Data importing, pre-processing and descriptive statistics have all been completed. ML methodology has been applied. Processes are correct. |
| 60-69 | A very good understanding of most of the processes. Import, pre-processing and descriptive statistics completed. ML methodology applied. Processes mostly correct and justified. |
| 50-59 | Satisfactory understanding of most processes. Some import, pre-processing and descriptive statistics. ML methodology mostly applied. Some processes correct and justified. |
| 40-49 | Insufficient understanding, pre-processing, statistics and methodology. |
| 30-39 | Underdeveloped. |
| 20-29 | Minimal. |
| 0-19 | None. |

## What the top bands actually ask for

1. **Data importing** shown and correct (evidence: load cell, shape, dtypes screenshot).
2. **Pre-processing** complete and *correct* (every cleaning decision justified and verified by counts).
3. **Descriptive statistics** explicit (summary table, distributions, skew, correlations), not only plots.
4. **Correct ML methodology**: scaling before distance-based clustering, principled k/eps selection, validation indices, stability, no leakage of labels or post-hoc features.
5. **All processes and solutions correct**: reproducible, consistent numbers notebook ↔ report.
6. **90+ "judgement"**: genuine alternatives, sensitivity checks, honest limits, business-relevant choice of final model.
